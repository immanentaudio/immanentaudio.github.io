IA De-Esser

Free · De-Essing & Resonance Suppression

IA De-Esser is a free de-essing and resonance suppression plugin — 4 filter slots for sibilance and harshness taming.

Windows and macOS-compatible (VST3 and AU)

What It Does

  • Seamless de-essing: simply turn on a Slot in De-Ess mode and let it tame sibilance automatically
  • Auto Mode: Lock in your Low-Cut and Hi-Cut, and let the plugin intelligently choose your depth and Q for appropriate harshness taming
  • Smart Mode: The plugin hunts for any part of the spectrum that needs taming, and sends your current slot there to handle it
  • View live readouts of Atk/Rel/Depth/Q

~Cut only the harshness you want to get rid of, and easily avoid an over-processed feel~

~Use Smart Mode as a 'set-and-forget,' or follow where it travels to help you surgically set your Low-Cuts and Hi-Cuts~

~Zero added DAW Latency~
