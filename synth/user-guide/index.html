IA Synth User Guide

Synthesizer, chord and rhythm studio, and EQ in one instrument.

Synth Chords EQ

Overview

IA Synth is a polyphonic synthesizer with a chord and rhythm studio and a console-style EQ built in. It has three pages, switched with the tabs at the top.

  • SYNTH: 120 presets and all the controls.
  • CHORDS: genre-specific chord and progression suggestions, a piano roll, rhythm grooves, and MIDI drag-and-drop to your DAW.
  • EQ: a 5-band EQ with HPF/LPF and presets.

Getting started

  1. Insert IA Synth on an instrument track.
  2. Pick a preset with the arrows or the preset name at the top.
  3. Play from your keyboard, the on-screen keys, or your computer keyboard.
  4. Open CHORDS to write a progression and drag into your DAW.

The keyboard

Click to play, or use your computer keyboard. Z and X (or the < and > buttons) move the computer keys down or up an octave.

The SYNTH page

Two oscillators, a sub and noise feed the filter and amp. Effects, stereo tools and the performance controls sit below. Sections that are switched off are dimmed.

The CHORDS page

Write progressions without leaving the plugin. Everything follows the key, so the whole library works in any key.

Key, mode and genre

ControlWhat it does
Key / Major-MinorThe key.
Genre12 genres, each with their own chords, progressions and grooves.
BASS NOTEAdds the root note in the bass.

Chords and progressions

ControlWhat it does
Chord tilesHold to hear. The top button picks the chord colour and the lower one the inversion (hover the list to hear each).
ProgressionsClick to loop a preview; click again to stop.
DragDrag any tile onto the roll, or straight into your DAW as MIDI.
+ SAVESaves the chord selected in the roll, or the roll's progression, to this genre. Right-click a saved tile to rename or delete it.

The piano roll

ControlWhat it does
ROLL / RHYTHMShow the roll alone, or the rhythm editor under it.
Length / Snap1 to 16 bars / grid size.
Time signatureAuto follows your DAW, or pick one.
PLAY WITH DAWPlays the roll in time whenever the DAW plays.
Play / Pause / Back to startPreview the roll while the DAW is stopped.
CLEAR NOTES / CLEAR RHYTHMEmpty the roll / switch the rhythm off.
Undo, Redo, Copy, PasteAlso Cmd (Ctrl) Z, C, X, V, and D.

Editing: double-click the grid to add a note (keep holding and drag to set its length), drag a note to move it, drag its right edge to resize, double-click it to delete. Drag on empty grid to select.

Rhythm

ControlWhat it does
Groove tilesClick to load a groove for this genre and meter. Click the lit one to turn it off.
CHORD / BASS lanesClick a step to add or remove a hit, drag to hold it, shift-click to accent.
Pattern / SWINGOne- or two-bar pattern / how much the off-beats lag.
+ SAVESave the grid as your own groove.

The roll holds the harmony and the rhythm decides when it plays. Each hit plays whatever chord is current at that moment.

Playing with your DAW

  • Bar 1 of the clip sits where you press play in the DAW, or on the loop start when the DAW is looping.
  • Switching PLAY WITH DAW on mid-song joins at the current position.
  • While the roll has notes, the track's own MIDI is muted.
  • Once you drag the clip into your DAW, PLAY WITH DAW turns off, so the notes aren't doubled.

The EQ page

A 5-band EQ on the synth's output (with HPF and LPF). Changing synth presets doesn’t change EQ settings.

Curves

ControlWhat it does
Curve buttons12 preset EQs.
+ SAVESave your own. Right-click it to rename, update or delete.
EQ INBypass switch.
MATCHKeeps the loudness level, so you hear the tone change, not a volume jump.

The display

  • Drag a dot to move that band: left and right for frequency, up and down for gain.
  • Mouse wheel over a dot changes its width (Q). Double-click a dot to return it to the curve.
  • The filled trace is the sound going in; the outline is what comes out.

The bands

ControlWhat it does
HIGH PASSCuts lows below FREQ (18 dB/oct). Fully left is off.
LOW / AIRShelves for the bottom and top end.
LOW MID / MID / PRESENCEBell bands for the low, middle, and high mids.
GAIN / FREQ / Q±12 dB / band frequency / width. Q centered is Auto and follows the gain.
LOW PASSCuts highs above FREQ (12 dB/oct). Fully right is off.
